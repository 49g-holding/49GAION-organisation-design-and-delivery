# DR-0002 · Organisation Design and Delivery product: the readings of 49GAION/DR-0041

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION
- **Source:** `49GAION/DR-0041`, the complete definition of 49GAION (`49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md`), approved by the governing seat on 25 October 2026, and its direction of the same day (translated): "Go to all the repositories; remove nothing; where something is contrary to it, bring it into line, and put this definition into them."
- **Relation:** builds on DR-0001; it supersedes nothing and removes nothing.

## Decision

1. **The complete definition governs.** The complete definition of 49GAION, approved by its governing seat on 25 October 2026, is `49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md` in `github.com/49g-holding/49GAION` (`49GAION/DR-0041`). Where this repository differs from it, the definition governs, and this repository is read with it until its next version brings it into line. Nothing here is removed.
2. **A pattern of the Library.** This text is a pattern of the 49GAION Library, the greatest asset of every `[ORG]AION`. An organisation takes it, personalises it or builds something better, and what it takes is held in its own Library, at its governance level, as its own rules (the definition, see 9 and 14). 49GAION writes principles and the few safeguards that always hold (the definition, see 20). Where this text sets a detail or a way of working, that detail is the pattern's default: the organisation's `[ORG]AION` decides it at the time and may change it, within the safeguards and with the approval the organisation's own rules require.
3. **The hub of the operation.** Every rule here that names `[ORG]AIOC-OCO`, the Operating Core Office, or the human hub is read with the definition (see 19): the Top Management Group, the first AIOC under the core, is the hub of the operation, both the human hub and the AI hub, and builds everything in the operation, or plans it, or puts its tools in place. 49GAION requires no separate AI hub unit; where an organisation has one, `[ORG]AIOC-OCO` does this work under Top Management. Where the organisation has none, "never by `[ORG]AIOC-OCO` alone" reads "never by any AI alone".
4. **Panel and back office.** The panel is the unit's interface: the organisation's own, written in code, made from the 49GAION patterns, from which every task of the unit can be controlled. The back office is the AI chat workspace where the unit's AI works: an authorised interface too, through the same controls, and the keeper of the panel, also after the panel is built, so no separate admin back office is written. Each covers the other: if the panel fails, the work goes on from the back office, which can rebuild it. The back office is never the route of a human port, a stop door, a signature or the accountability path (the definition, see 17).
5. **Positions.** Every human seat here is a position, never a person. The governance seat appoints Top Management directly and keeps the final right over who holds any position; Top Management fills the positions below it in its own mechanism. One person may hold several positions where the law allows; the positions stay distinct (the definition, see 6).
6. **The next level.** Blueprint §12 calls the choice of which positions an AI fills "the next level, the path of an `[ORG]AION`". Read with the definition, that level is the `[ORG]AION`'s own design work: the first proposal from 49GAION's patterns, then Structural Adaptation. How human and how much AI the organisation is, is prepared by the `[ORG]AION` and decided by the governance seat (the definition, see 13 and 15). This product is one pattern of the Library that an `[ORG]AION` may take, personalise or replace.
7. **Withdrawn names.** Upstream draft 9 names the AION Builder, its six stages and Ionize. They are withdrawn (`49GAION/DR-0032`), and the draft is moot; it is kept, unproposed.
8. **Powered by 49GAION.** An organisation that takes this part, or any other item of 49GAION, carries "Powered by 49GAION" on all its products (the definition, see 11).
9. **The version.** Unreleased. The product blueprint stays at `v0.1.0`, its pins do not change, and the Library register does not move. The readings are folded into the text at its next version, which reaches the Library only by a pull request that moves the pin, on the governing seat's word.
